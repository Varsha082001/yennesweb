Customized Manufacturing Solution in TallyPrime

A customized manufacturing solution in TallyPrime designed to support end-to-end manufacturing operations across different industry verticals. The solution focuses on improving process visibility, operational efficiency, cost control and decision-making.

Key Manufacturing Processes

1. Production Planning

Plan and manage production activities efficiently with better visibility into manufacturing requirements and processes.

2. Raw Material Control

Monitor raw material requirements, availability and consumption to maintain better control over inventory.

3. Shop Floor Operations

Improve visibility and control over shop floor activities and manufacturing operations.

4. Quality Checks

Support quality control processes and ensure products meet the required quality standards.

5. Inventory Management

Manage inventory effectively across raw materials, production and finished goods.

6. Dispatch & Logistics

Streamline dispatch activities and logistics for efficient movement of finished products.

Solutions Across Verticals

The manufacturing solution can be positioned for multiple industry sectors:

⚙️ Engineering

Support manufacturing and engineering operations with improved production, inventory and process control.

🧪 Chemicals & Pharma

Manage manufacturing processes, material control, inventory and quality-related activities.

🧵 Textiles

Support textile manufacturing operations from production planning through inventory and dispatch.

🍴 Food & Beverage

Improve control over production, inventory, quality checks and distribution processes.

💻 Electronics

Support electronics manufacturing with structured production, inventory and operational control.

🪑 Furniture & More

Provide manufacturing and inventory management capabilities for furniture and other manufacturing businesses.

Business Benefits

👁️ End-to-End Visibility

Gain better visibility across the complete manufacturing process, from planning and raw materials to dispatch.

⚡ Operational Efficiency

Streamline manufacturing activities and improve operational control.

💰 Cost Optimization

Improve control over materials, inventory and manufacturing processes to support cost optimization.
